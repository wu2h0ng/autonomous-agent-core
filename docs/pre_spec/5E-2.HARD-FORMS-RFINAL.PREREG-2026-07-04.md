## Scope

This r-final tests the hard non-enumerable functional-form route after:

- zero-shot 5e-2 calibration found no freeze-ready region;
- ordinary threshold/saturation/ratio forms had signal but pair screening
  remained too strong;
- hard `bandpass_product` forms found a calibration candidate region on seeds
  `30..33`;
- hard forms fresh scored gate returned `MET` on disjoint seeds `40..45`.

This r-final asks a narrower question:

> In the hard bandpass-form arena, do supplied typed form proposals give the
> invariant verifier access to causal structure that finite pair-product
> screening and random hard-form proposals fail to recover, while collapsing
> under Condition-B?

## Decision Rule

`MET` iff all conditions hold on r-final seeds:

- `median_s_proposed_hard_forms >= 0.80`;
- `median_s_pair_screening <= 0.60`;
- `median_s_random_hard_forms <= 0.20`;
- proposed hard forms beat pair screening in at least 80% of r-final seeds;
- proposed hard forms beat random hard forms in at least 80% of r-final seeds;
- Condition-B proposed hard forms do not exceed random hard forms by more than
  `0.10`.

`INVALID(CONDITION-B-ANOMALY)` iff Condition-B proposed hard forms exceed random
hard forms by more than `0.10`.

Otherwise `NULL`.
