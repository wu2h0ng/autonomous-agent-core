## Scope

This is a fresh scored gate for the hard `bandpass_product` forms arena after
calibration found a candidate region on seeds `30..33`. Scoring used disjoint
fresh seeds `40..45`.

This is not r-final. A separate docs preregistration file did not exist before
the run; the frozen elements were the runner constants and test-guarded decision
logic. Therefore this result may support the 5e-2 mechanism ladder, but it does
not authorize autonomy claims, product claims, route promotion, or C6/C7 changes.

## Frozen Rule

`MET` iff all hold:

- `median_s_proposed_hard_forms >= 0.80`
- `median_s_pair_screening <= 0.60`
- `median_s_random_hard_forms <= 0.20`
- proposed hard forms beat pair screening in at least 80% of fresh seeds
- proposed hard forms beat random hard forms in at least 80% of fresh seeds
- Condition-B proposed hard forms do not exceed random hard forms by more than
  `0.10`

`INVALID` iff Condition-B proposed hard forms exceed random hard forms by more
than `0.10`; otherwise `NULL`.
