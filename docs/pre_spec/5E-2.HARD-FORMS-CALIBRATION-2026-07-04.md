## Claim Tested

The first functional-forms arena had a healthy typed-form signal, but finite
pair-product screening remained too strong. This calibration redesigned the
arena so the true forms are bandpass-gated products: the signal variable matters
only when a gate variable is near a narrow band. That preserves verifier health
for supplied typed forms while making raw pair products poor proxies.

This is calibration only. It authorizes at most a fresh-seed freeze/scored gate,
not a verdict, r-final, autonomy claim, product claim, route promotion, or C6/C7
change.
