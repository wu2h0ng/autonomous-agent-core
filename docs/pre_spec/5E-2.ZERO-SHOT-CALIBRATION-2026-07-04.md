## Claim Tested

CWM-LEARN-5e was `MET-weak`: frozen data-blind proposals reached the oracle
ceiling, but a cheap data-screening baseline could also reach it. 5e-2 zero-shot
calibration asked whether there is a small-data region where:

- the candidate set is fixed before any data exists;
- later data is used only for verification and cheap screening;
- frozen zero-shot proposals remain verifiable;
- cheap screening collapses enough to make the knowledge channel unique.

This is calibration only. It does not authorize a freeze verdict, r-final,
autonomy claim, product claim, route promotion, or C6/C7 change.

## Readout

No legal freeze-ready region was found.

At `n_train=38/40`, the zero-shot union has real signal, but cheap screening is
not collapsed enough. At `n_train<=36`, the verifier/zero-shot side is unstable
and does not produce a reliable win over random or screening. At larger sample
sizes, screening catches up.

The zero-shot branch therefore should not be frozen by lowering thresholds,
reusing favorable seeds, or rewording the claim. The legitimate next 5e-2 route
is the non-enumerable functional-form branch, or a redesigned low-sample
verifier that is frozen before seeing fresh scoring seeds.
